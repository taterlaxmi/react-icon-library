Quality Benchmark: Why 2 KB Vector is Better Than 53 KB Autotrace

In earlier versions, running image tracing on PNG/JPEG produced giant, pixel-approximated paths. Here is why true vectors are superior in production:

✅ Clean Scalable Vectors (Current)

Uses pure cubic bezier math. Renders with pixel-perfect clarity at 16px, 128px, or on 4K retina displays with 0 pixelation. Background is 100% transparent. Bundle size is just 8.6 KB.

❌ Autotraced Bitmaps (Old)

Approximated pixels into thousands of jagged polygon slivers (53 KB Drinkprime). Produced an opaque solid background box that broke dark mode, with muddy color bands.
